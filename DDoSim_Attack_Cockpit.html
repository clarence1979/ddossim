DDoSim — Attack Cockpit

SAFE SIMULATION

You are the attacker. This is a safe model of a DDoS attack for learning — no real packets are sent, and every target is a fictional lab machine on a reserved address range.

◧ Left World map — bots flooding your target.
◱ Top-right Your Kali terminal — type commands here.
◲ Bottom-right The victim server — watch it struggle and fall over.
  1. Type targets to see who you can attack.
  2. Scan one, e.g. nmap 203.0.113.55.
  3. Launch it: python3 ddosim.py --target 203.0.113.55 --bots 300
  4. Watch the map turn red and the victim crash. Press Ctrl+C to stop.

Read the tool with cat ddosim.py or nano ddosim.py · defend with --mitigation · try mitm or sqli for the man-in-the-middle and SQL-injection labs · help lists everything.
